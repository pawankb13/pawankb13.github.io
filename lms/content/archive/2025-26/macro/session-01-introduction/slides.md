# Introduction to Macroeconomics

Macroeconomics · Session 1

---

## What macroeconomics studies

- The economy as a whole
- Growth, inflation, unemployment, interest rates and exchange rates
- The role of monetary and fiscal policy

---

## Three key indicators

| Indicator | Measures |
|---|---|
| Real GDP growth | Change in output |
| CPI inflation | Change in consumer prices |
| Unemployment rate | Share of the labour force without work |
