# Demand and Supply

Managerial Economics · Session 2

---

## The law of demand

- Other things equal, a higher price lowers quantity demanded
- A change in price moves us **along** the curve
- A change in income, tastes or related prices **shifts** the curve

---

## Market equilibrium

$$Q_d(P^*) = Q_s(P^*)$$

- At the equilibrium price, buyers and sellers agree on quantity
- Surplus pushes the price down; shortage pushes it up

---
