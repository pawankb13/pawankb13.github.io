# Framing a Research Question

Research Courses · Session 1

---

## From topic to question

| Stage | Example |
|---|---|
| Topic | Financial inclusion |
| Focus | Bank accounts and women |
| Question | Did account ownership raise savings among rural women? |

---

## A good question is

1. **Answerable** with data you can get
2. **Specific** about who, what and when
3. **Relevant** to a real debate or policy
4. **New** in some clear way

---

## Workshop

Write your research question in one sentence. Swap with a partner. Check it against the four tests.

Note:
Fifteen minutes. Collect three questions to discuss with the whole class.
