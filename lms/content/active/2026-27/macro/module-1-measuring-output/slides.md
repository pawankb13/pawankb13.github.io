## Gross Domestic Product

GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a given period.

- **Market value:** adds goods using prices
- **Final:** counts only goods sold to end users
- **Within a country:** location, not nationality

---

## The expenditure approach

$$Y = C + I + G + NX$$

| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| C | Household consumption |
| I | Business investment and housing |
| G | Government purchases |
| NX | Exports minus imports |

---

## Nominal and real GDP

|||

- **Nominal GDP** uses current prices
- **Real GDP** uses base-year prices
- Real GDP shows changes in output alone

|||

$$\text{Deflator} = \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}}{\text{Real GDP}} \times 100$$
