## Why managers need economics

- Every business decision is a choice under scarcity
- Prices, costs and competitors limit what a firm can do
- Economics gives a simple, testable way to think through these choices

---

## Four principles of decisions

1. People face trade-offs
2. Cost is what you give up
3. Rational people think at the margin
4. People respond to incentives

Note:
Ask students for one trade-off they faced this week. Link "thinking at the margin" to the decision to sell one more unit.

---

## Thinking at the margin

A firm should produce one more unit when

$$MR > MC$$

|||

**Example**

- A café sells one more coffee for ₹150
- The extra milk, beans and cup cost ₹60
- The extra unit adds ₹90 to profit
