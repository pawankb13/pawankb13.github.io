### Start here

- Kahneman, D. (2011). Thinking, Fast and Slow. Farrar, Straus and Giroux. Part IV, "Choices", covers prospect theory, the endowment effect, framing and loss aversion in plain language.
- Thaler, R. H. (2015). Misbehaving: The Making of Behavioral Economics. W. W. Norton. The story of the endowment effect and mental accounting, told by the person who named them.

### Prospect theory and the value function

- [Kahneman, D. and Tversky, A. (1979). Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk. Econometrica, 47(2), 263–291](https://doi.org/10.2307/1914185). The original paper. Read sections 1 and 3.
- [Tversky, A. and Kahneman, D. (1992). Advances in Prospect Theory: Cumulative Representation of Uncertainty. Journal of Risk and Uncertainty, 5(4), 297–323](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00122574). The source of α = 0.88, λ = 2.25 and the decision weight function.

### Loss aversion

- [Brown, A. L., Imai, T., Vieider, F. M. and Camerer, C. F. (2024). Meta-analysis of Empirical Estimates of Loss Aversion. Journal of Economic Literature, 62(2), 485–516](https://doi.org/10.1257/jel.20221698). 607 estimates from 150 articles. The mean λ is about 1.96.
- [Pope, D. G. and Schweitzer, M. E. (2011). Is Tiger Woods Loss Averse? Persistent Bias in the Face of Experience, Competition, and High Stakes. American Economic Review, 101(1), 129–157](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.101.1.129). The golf case.
- [Odean, T. (1998). Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses? Journal of Finance, 53(5), 1775–1798](https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00072). The disposition effect and the purchase price as a reference point.

### Endowment effect and status quo bias

- [Kahneman, D., Knetsch, J. L. and Thaler, R. H. (1991). Anomalies: The Endowment Effect, Loss Aversion, and Status Quo Bias. Journal of Economic Perspectives, 5(1), 193–206](https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.5.1.193). Short, readable and the best single reading for this part.
- [Kahneman, D., Knetsch, J. L. and Thaler, R. H. (1990). Experimental Tests of the Endowment Effect and the Coase Theorem. Journal of Political Economy, 98(6), 1325–1348](https://doi.org/10.1086/261737). The mugs experiment.
- [Samuelson, W. and Zeckhauser, R. (1988). Status Quo Bias in Decision Making. Journal of Risk and Uncertainty, 1(1), 7–59](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00055564). The paper that named the bias.
- [Johnson, E. J., Hershey, J., Meszaros, J. and Kunreuther, H. (1993). Framing, Probability Distortions, and Insurance Decisions. Journal of Risk and Uncertainty, 7(1), 35–51](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01065313). The New Jersey and Pennsylvania car insurance case.
- [Income Tax Department. New Tax vs Old Tax Regime FAQs](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/new-tax-vs-old-tax-regime-faqs). The official note on the new regime as the default from FY 2023-24.

### Gain and loss framing

- [Tversky, A. and Kahneman, D. (1981). The Framing of Decisions and the Psychology of Choice. Science, 211(4481), 453–458](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7455683). The disease problem, the jacket and calculator, and the theatre ticket.
- [Fryer, R. G., Levitt, S. D., List, J. and Sadoff, S. (2022). Enhancing the Efficacy of Teacher Incentives through Framing: A Field Experiment. American Economic Journal: Economic Policy, 14(4), 269–299](https://doi.org/10.1257/pol.20190287). The Chicago Heights teachers case.
- [Hossain, T. and List, J. A. (2012). The Behavioralist Visits the Factory: Increasing Productivity Using Simple Framing Manipulations. Management Science, 58(12), 2151–2167](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.1120.1544). The Wanlida factory case.

### Segregating and integrating outcomes

- [Thaler, R. H. (1985). Mental Accounting and Consumer Choice. Marketing Science, 4(3), 199–214](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.4.3.199). The four rules of hedonic editing.
- [Thaler, R. H. (1999). Mental Accounting Matters. Journal of Behavioral Decision Making, 12(3), 183–206](https://doi.org/10.1002/(SICI)1099-0771(199909)12:3<183::AID-BDM318>3.0.CO;2-F). A later, broader review for those who want more.

### Business situations used in class

- [Outlook Business. ₹5, ₹10 Pack Maths No Longer Adds Up For FMCG Majors](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/fmcg-5-10-rupees-packs-costs-rising-brands-margins-declining-shrinkflation). Parle-G and shrinkflation.
- [Reserve Bank of Australia (1995). Implications of the Barings Collapse for Bank Supervisors](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/bulletin/1995/nov/1.html). How losses grew to £827 million.
- [Associated Press (2011). Netflix loses 800,000 US subscribers in tough 3Q](https://phys.org/news/2011-10-netflix-subscribers-tough-3q.html). The 2011 price rise.
- [Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/social-awareness/consumer-education/the-guidelines-for-prevention-and-regulation-of-dark-patterns-2023). The 13 dark patterns, including basket sneaking.
- [US Federal Trade Commission. Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees](https://www.ftc.gov/node/88176). Total price upfront for hotels and live-event tickets.
