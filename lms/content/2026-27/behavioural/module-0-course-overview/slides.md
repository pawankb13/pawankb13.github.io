# Behavioural Economics for Managers

Module 0 · Course overview

Where the standard model of decision-making breaks, and what a manager does about it

---
<!-- layout: callout -->
## What this course is for
### The standard model, and where it breaks

> Standard economics assumes people decide with full information, unlimited attention and perfect self-control. Real customers, employees and managers do not.

This course studies how people actually decide, where the standard model breaks, and what a manager does about it.
