# The IS-LM Model

Macroeconomics · Module 5

---

## Why IS-LM?

- In the short run, prices are sticky
- Output is set by aggregate demand
- IS-LM links the goods market and the money market

---

## The IS curve: goods market

$$Y = C(Y - T) + I(r) + G$$

- A lower interest rate raises investment
- Higher investment raises income
- So IS slopes **downward** in (Y, r) space

---

## The LM curve: money market

$$\frac{M}{P} = L(r, Y)$$

- Higher income raises money demand
- With fixed money supply, the interest rate must rise
- So LM slopes **upward**

---

## Equilibrium

|||

The economy settles where both markets clear at the same time: income $Y^*$ and interest rate $r^*$.

## Policy in IS-LM

| Policy | Curve | Income | Interest rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiscal expansion (G up) | IS shifts right | Rises | Rises |
| Monetary expansion (M up) | LM shifts right | Rises | Falls |
| Both together | Both shift right | Rises | Uncertain |

Note:
Ask: why does fiscal expansion crowd out investment? Link to RBI and Union Budget timing.
